Meet the founder

One person. Infinite tiny things.

Marion is the founder, designer, bead-sorter, soil-mixer, quality inspector and customer-service department of this entire operation. No board meetings, no spreadsheets. Just patience, a very steady hand and a lot of little jars.

Every bracelet is threaded to match you, and every mini garden is potted with a small amount of encouragement. Nothing is mass-produced, because Marion cannot be mass-produced (we asked).

  • Made one at a time, never in a factory

Questions

Can I customise everything?

Pretty much. Colours, bead sizes, lengths, letters, pot styles and plant types. If you can imagine it, Marion can probably bead it or pot it.

How long does an order take?

Good things take time, and tiny things take slightly more time. Ask Marion for an estimate when you order.

Will my mini garden survive?

Yes, with a little light, a little water and a lot of love. Marion will include care tips so it thrives.

Is this website a joke?

The website is a joke. The jewellery and gardens are very real and very lovely. Both are made with sincere affection.
